## Page= `/market`

- [ ] The **left side-bar** displays list of career clusters. 
		- Source: [Louisiana Career Clusters](https://www.laworks.net/Tableau/LMI_Tableau.asp?TableauCode=LouisianaCareerClusters/Homepage?:origin=card_share_link&:embed=%20yes&:toolbar=no&Title=Louisiana%20Career%20Clusters)
		- Career cluster definition retrieved from: [CareerTech](https://careertech.org/career-clusters)
		- The options: `who's hiring`, `who's hiring more`, and `who pays more` are used to sort the career cluster list. The numbers shown are based on statewide data. For example, cluster: `Digital technology` pays the highest in the LA state with a reported median salary of `$117,120`. 
			- `who's hiring`: Number of job postings
			- `who's hiring more`: Rate of job postings (30 days) 
			- `who pays more`: Median salary

- [ ] The bottom of the page displays two matrix: Student enrollment (enrolled students) and Market Demand (based on job postings). 
	- I used a simple linear equation to compute a reductive score for each `region-career_cluster`. The normalized values are calculated at the region level. 
	- These are not the final models. I just wanted to give an idea how we can model `market demand` and `workforce supply` across career clusters for each region.
